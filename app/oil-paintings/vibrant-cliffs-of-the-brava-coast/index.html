Vibrant Cliffs of the Brava Coast

Vibrant Cliffs of the Brava Coast (Inspired by P. Molina) captures warm twilight on a rugged Mediterranean coast. Painted in oil on an 18 × 24 cm canvas, golden hues merge with deep blues, evoking serenity at day's end. Layered brushstrokes enhance the coastline's charm, inviting the viewer to savor nature's fleeting beauty.
