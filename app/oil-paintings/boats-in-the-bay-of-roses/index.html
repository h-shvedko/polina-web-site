Boats in the Bay of Roses

Boats in the Bay of Roses (Inspired by P. Molina) depicts a tranquil moment of repose. Painted in oil on a 30 × 24 cm canvas, gentle brushstrokes and soft sunlight create an atmosphere of warmth and serenity. Muted hues and subtle details invite viewers to pause and embrace the painting's calming aura.
