Forget-Me-Nots in Glass

This pastel painting delicately portrays a bouquet of vibrant blue forget-me-nots arranged in a simple glass jar. The soft strokes bring life to the delicate flowers, while the warm, textured background adds depth and contrast. Gentle highlights on the jar and leaves evoke a sense of light and airiness, enhancing the painting's natural elegance. This piece captures the timeless beauty of a humble yet heartfelt floral arrangement.
