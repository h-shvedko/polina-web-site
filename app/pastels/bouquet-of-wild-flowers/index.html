Bouquet of wild flowers

This pastel painting captures the delicate beauty of a wildflower bouquet arranged in a soft, rounded vase. The vibrant yellows, pinks, and whites of the flowers stand out against a warm, muted background, creating a sense of natural elegance. The subtle highlights on the vase add a touch of light, completing this serene and timeless composition.
