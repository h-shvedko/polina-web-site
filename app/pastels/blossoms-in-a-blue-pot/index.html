Blossoms in a Blue Pot

This pastel painting captures the vibrant beauty of pink hydrangeas in full bloom, arranged in a simple blue pot. The soft blending of textures and colors highlights the lush greenery and delicate petals. With a muted, warm background, the composition exudes a timeless charm, inviting you to savor the serenity and natural elegance of the scene.
