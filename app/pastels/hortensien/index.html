Hortensien

This pastel painting showcases a vibrant arrangement of hydrangeas in full bloom, set in a simple yet elegant pot. The rich purples and blues of the flowers contrast beautifully with the lush green leaves, while the soft gray tones of the background enhance their vividness. The delicate play of light and shadow on the tiled surface adds depth and realism to the composition. This piece captures the quiet beauty of a garden moment, evoking a sense of serenity and grace.
