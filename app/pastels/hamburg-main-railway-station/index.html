Hamburg, Main railway station

This pastel painting showcases the iconic Hamburg Main Railway Station, a masterpiece of historic architecture set against a dramatic sky. The intricate details of the clock tower and arched glass roofs are brought to life with rich textures and vibrant pastel tones. The soft hues of the evening sky add a sense of tranquility, balancing the grandeur of this bustling urban landmark. It's a tribute to Hamburg's vibrant history and timeless charm.
