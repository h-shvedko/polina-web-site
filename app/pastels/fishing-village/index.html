Fishing village

This pastel painting captures the charm of a quaint waterfront village. Vibrant colors bring life to the houses, blooming trees, and moored boats, while their soft reflections dance across the water's surface. The expressive strokes and dreamlike atmosphere evoke the beauty of a serene harbor scene. This piece celebrates the timeless allure of small-town coastal life with a touch of artistic whimsy.
