Café on a Sunny Corner

This watercolor painting captures the lively charm of a bustling café with its vibrant red awnings and the gentle shade of nearby trees. The soft, flowing brushstrokes bring the warmth of a sunny day to life, while the subtle interplay of light and shadow enhances the building's character. The scene invites viewers to pause and soak in the relaxed ambiance of a perfect moment in a quaint urban setting.

In a private collection
