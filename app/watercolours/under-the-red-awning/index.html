Under the Red Awning

This watercolor and ink painting depicts the cozy charm of a café on a quiet street corner, framed by a vibrant red awning. Silhouetted figures pass by, adding life and movement to the scene. The soft washes of color and subtle reflections on the ground evoke the tranquil beauty of a rainy urban afternoon. The composition invites viewers to pause and enjoy the warmth of a fleeting moment.
