By the Old Watermill

This watercolor and ink painting captures the tranquil charm of a historic watermill scene. The soft hues of purple and blue reflect off the calm water, while the intricate architectural details of the mill and bridge are brought to life through delicate linework. A lone cyclist crossing the bridge adds a touch of modern life to the peaceful, timeless setting. This piece evokes a sense of quiet nostalgia and connection to the past.
