Sunny Village Square

This watercolor and ink painting captures the warmth and charm of a quiet village square bathed in sunlight. The rustic stone buildings, adorned with red-tiled roofs, create a cozy and inviting atmosphere. Soft washes of color and fine architectural details bring life to the scene, while figures casually strolling add a sense of everyday simplicity. The artwork evokes the timeless beauty of rural life.
