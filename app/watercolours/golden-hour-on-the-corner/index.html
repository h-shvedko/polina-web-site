Golden Hour on the Corner

This vibrant watercolor painting depicts a sunlit corner building, its elegant balconies and warm facade glowing under the soft light of the golden hour. Dynamic brushstrokes and splashes of color bring energy and depth to the scene, while the interplay of shadow and light highlights the building's intricate architectural details. The composition invites viewers to experience the charm and vibrancy of urban life at its most picturesque moment.

In a private collection
