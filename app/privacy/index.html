3. Your cookie choice (localStorage)

On your first visit, a notice at the bottom of the screen asks whether you allow Google Analytics. Your choice ("Accept" or "Decline") is stored in your browser's local storage (localStorage, entry cookie_consent_v2) so that the notice does not appear on every page. This information does not leave your device. Storing it is strictly necessary to manage your consent (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG; Art. 6(1)(c) in conjunction with Art. 7(1) GDPR). You can delete the entry at any time in your browser settings.

4. Google Analytics 4 (only with your consent)

If you consent, this website uses Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). Before you consent, no script is loaded from Google and no analytics cookie is set. The website uses Google's Consent Mode v2: all types of storage are denied by default; after "Accept", only analytics_storage is granted; advertising features are not enabled.

Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*, stored for up to 2 years) and collects pseudonymous usage data, for example the pages visited, time on the site, approximate location (country, city), device type, browser, referrer, clicks on the e-mail links (event contact_click) and starts of the video (hero_video_play). Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses; within the EU, Google uses them only briefly to derive the approximate location. The data are deleted after [retention period set in Google Analytics, e.g. 2 months].

The purpose is to understand how visitors use the website in order to improve it. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. A data processing agreement is in place with Google. It cannot be ruled out that data are transferred to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). Google has also concluded standard contractual clauses.

Withdrawal: You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future: click "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page and choose "Decline". Google Analytics is then disabled and the _ga cookies are deleted. More information: Google's privacy policy.

5. YouTube video on the home page

The header of the home page shows a muted background video from YouTube, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The video is embedded in privacy-enhanced mode from www.youtube-nocookie.com and starts automatically when the home page loads (not when your device is set to "reduce motion"). A connection to Google's servers is therefore made as soon as the page loads, and Google receives in particular your IP address, the page visited and technical details of your browser and device. According to YouTube, privacy-enhanced mode sets no cookies before you interact with the video; the video may, however, store data in your browser's storage. A transfer to Google LLC in the USA is possible (see the Data Privacy Framework above).

The purpose is an appealing presentation of the home page. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is presenting my work. Where information is stored on or read from your device, this is based on § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG. You can pause the video at any time with the button on the video. More information: Google's privacy policy.

6. Contact by e-mail

If you write me an e-mail, I process your e-mail address, your name (if given) and the content of your message in order to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request aims at a contract (for example acquiring a work), otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering requests). E-mails are received through Gmail (Google Ireland Limited). I delete your data as soon as they are no longer needed to handle your request, unless statutory retention periods apply.

7. Links to social networks

The icons for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Etsy are plain links. Only when you click one does the page of that provider open, which then processes data under its own responsibility.
